# Research Summary

## Project Topic
**AI-assisted financial news intelligence for investment research teams**

The project explores how generative AI can support investment-research analysts by reducing the manual effort required to review, classify, summarize, and prioritize financial news and market-related text.

## Business Problem
Investment analysts may need to review large volumes of financial headlines, news, and market commentary.

Manual review can be:
- Time-consuming
- Difficult to prioritize consistently
- Prone to missed high-risk information
- Repetitive across many sources

## AI Opportunity
AI can support analysts by:
- Identifying the financial target or company
- Classifying the business area and topic
- Detecting sentiment
- Assigning a priority level
- Producing a concise factual summary

The proposed solution is designed as an **analyst-assistance tool**. It is **not** intended to make autonomous investment decisions or execute trades.

## Research Direction
The project follows this staged approach:

**Offline Data → Dashboard & Analysis → AI POC → Evaluation & Monitoring → Live API → Hybrid Production Approach**

## Round 1 Scope
Round 1 focuses on:
- Offline FiQA data
- Tableau dashboard
- n8n + OpenAI proof of concept
- LangSmith monitoring
- Small benchmark evaluation
- Pilot recommendation
